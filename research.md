# AP Precalculus Research Notes (2026–2027 cycle)

Compiled September 2026 from College Board AP Central. Used to design the question bank,
practice modes, and marketing copy of this site. All practice questions on this site are
original and written from scratch — this file contains only public structural facts
(exam format, unit names, weightings), never copied questions.

## Exam format (AP Precalculus)
- Section I: Multiple Choice — 42 questions (Part A: 29 Qs, 65 min, no calculator; Part B: 13 Qs, 40 min, graphing calculator required), 62.5% of score (effective May 2027; College Board AP Central)
- Section II: Free Response — 4 questions × 3 parts (A/B/C), ~35% of score
- Mathematical practices: Procedural & Symbolic Fluency, Multiple Representations, Communication & Reasoning

## Units & MCQ weighting
- Unit 1: Polynomial and Rational Functions — 30–40%
- Unit 2: Exponential and Logarithmic Functions — 25–40%
- Unit 3: Trigonometric and Polar Functions — 30–35% (not yet on this site)
- Unit 4: Functions Involving Parameters, Vectors, and Matrices — not assessed on the AP exam

## Unit 1 topics
Polynomial functions and rates of change, complex zeros, end behavior, rational functions
(end behavior, holes, vertical asymptotes), equivalent representations.

## Unit 2 topics
Arithmetic/geometric sequences, exponential functions and manipulation, exponential modeling,
composition and inverses, logarithmic expressions/functions and manipulation, exponential and
logarithmic equations, logarithmic modeling, semi-log plots.
